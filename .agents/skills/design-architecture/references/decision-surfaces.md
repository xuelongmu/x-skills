# Consequential decision surfaces

Use this for unfamiliar territory or a suspected blind spot. Follow the evidence
that matters to this decision; do not fill a register for every possible concern.

- **Ownership:** Source of truth, write/correction authority, trust boundaries,
  public contracts, and whether work can remain inside an existing boundary.
- **State and time:** Atomicity, concurrency, stable intent identity, unknown
  outcomes, retries, reconciliation, and repair ownership.
- **Evolution:** Mixed versions, migration, rollback, accumulated state, provider
  replacement, and commitments that are expensive to reverse.
- **Operations:** Diagnostic evidence, cost or capacity bounds, alerts, replay,
  and who repairs partial or poisoned work.
- **Declared project constraints:** Authorization, credential custody, retention,
  compliance, portability, or other invariants the repository actually requires.

When the user cannot evaluate an unfamiliar area, supply settled facts and
realistic options. Turn relevant unknowns into a testable claim, a hazard, a
decision with a recommended default, or a bounded experiment. Explain the signal
that should change the recommendation.

A preference such as write ownership is a judgment decision; a claim about
provider behavior is testable. Recommend a reversible default where justified,
and name the missing evidence or expert judgment when no safe default exists.
