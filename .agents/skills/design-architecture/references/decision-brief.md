# Architecture decision brief

Keep the result concise enough for a driver to decide. Adapt headings to an
existing repository convention when one exists.

1. **Decision and why now** — the choice to make, affected actors and systems,
   success, safe failure, and reversal cost.
2. **Evidence and current system** — verified facts with repository or primary
   source references; identify important unknowns.
3. **Decision map** — fixed constraints, testable assumptions, judgment
   decisions, explicit deferrals, and accepted risks.
4. **Architectures considered** — materially different models, including the
   status quo or smallest change when credible.
5. **Consequence comparison** — the scenarios that discriminate between the
   designs; omit rows that do not matter.
6. **Recommendation** — dominant forces, complexity accepted and avoided, why
   alternatives lose, and invalidating assumptions.
7. **Proof and reversibility** — experiments or evidence required, reversible
   decisions, commitment points, migration and exit implications.
8. **Driver decision** — explicitly `Pending`, followed by the choices to accept,
   revise, reject, or gather evidence.

Do not include implementation code, a generic task breakdown, or ADR status.
After the driver accepts the direction, hand the brief to the repository's ADR
workflow rather than rewriting it as accepted history yourself.
